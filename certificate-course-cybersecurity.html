How the course is built

Read a little, then operate the thing

Every module keeps the same rhythm, so you always know where you are. Nothing here asks you to install software or set up a virtual machine — the labs are models that run in this page.

First

The brief

Plain-language writing on what the domain is, why attackers care about it, and which controls actually move the needle rather than just looking busy.

Then

The lab

An interactive model — a triage queue, a crypto bench, a Zero Trust decision engine, a prompt-injection sandbox — that answers your choices and shows its reasoning.

Last

The check

Three questions with worked explanations. Get two right and the module is marked complete. Reset and retry as often as you like.

Curriculum

Twelve modules, four tracks

Foundations build the vocabulary. Blue team teaches detection and response. Red team teaches offence under authorisation. AI security covers both sides of the model layer. The capstone puts the four together into work you could hand to a board.

Recall drill

Cyber crossword

Nineteen terms drawn from across the twelve modules. Tap a square, type a letter, and the grid moves with you. A word turns green the moment every letter in it is right.
