    How confidence is calculated

    Detection confidence is how precise each matching rule is: exact table or statement matches score 90–95%; heuristics such as LIS table names or SELECT-in-LOOP score 55–75%. Conversion confidence starts at 100 and subtracts 6 per High, 3 per Medium and 1 per Low finding, each weighted by its detection confidence. Above 80 = likely converts with light rework; 50–80 = planned remediation; below 50 = significant rework before conversion.

    Likelihood each record converts cleanly to a Business Partner, lowest first. Ready ≥ 80 · Review 50–79 · Blocked < 50.

    How confidence is calculated

    Each record starts at 100. Missing mandatory field −25 each, likely duplicate (not the golden record) −30, invalid country −15, invalid email −10, name over 40 characters −10, missing tax number −10. The dataset confidence is the average across records.

    How mapping and confidence work

    Each row is checked against a curated S/4HANA simplification knowledge base and a business-object model of ~60 objects across 18 S/4HANA areas (Sales, Procurement, Inventory, Warehouse, Transportation, Retail, Finance, Treasury, Controlling, Manufacturing, Quality, Maintenance, Master data, HR, Tax, Analytics, CX). The interface name, description, direction and source/target systems give the probable target: module, business object, released API, IDoc alternative and integration pattern for the technology. Retail abbreviations (ASN, DC, STO, SOH, GRN, POS, PLU…) are expanded first. Inferred rows have no ECC object; their confidence reflects how strongly the name points to one object. The knowledge base (Universal Journal, MATDOC, Business Partner/CVI, PRCD_ELEMENTS, SD status, credit management, output management, released APIs). Direct: object exists or has a 1:1 successor. Nearest: object is removed or not recommended, so the closest successor is proposed. When nothing matches exactly, similar names and business keywords (customer, invoice, stock…) are used, at lower confidence (50–60%). Always validate in an S/4HANA sandbox.
