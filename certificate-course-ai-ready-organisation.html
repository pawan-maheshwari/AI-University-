How the course is built

Read a little, then run the numbers

Every module keeps the same rhythm. The simulators run inside this page, and the labs are guided notebooks you open in Google Colab or Kaggle and drive with sliders. You never write code.

First

The brief

Plain-language writing on what the topic is, why it decides whether AI pays back, and the frameworks senior teams actually use.

Then

The simulator

A working model: a build-or-buy engine, an ROI curve, a data readiness radar, an operating-model chooser. Change the inputs and it explains the result.

Next

The lab

A no-code notebook on a real public dataset. Open it, choose Run all, move the sliders, and record what you find.

Last

The check

Three questions with worked explanations. Get two right and the module is marked complete. Each module also feeds one section of your capstone.

Curriculum

Six modules, three tracks

Strategy builds the vocabulary and the decision logic. Data and advantage covers where AI value actually comes from and how long it lasts. Execution turns a funded idea into measured, governed value at enterprise scale.

Capstone

Your AI business case

Build a board-ready case for one AI initiative in your own organisation, one section per module. Drafts are saved in this browser as you type. Copy the whole case when you are ready to share it with your sponsor.
