    How it was tested

    From proof of concept to pilot

    1. Swap the sample data for 2–3 seasons of real Kidswear sales, stock, prices, returns and comments. The pipeline runs unchanged.
    2. Re-score the forecast and size curves on real history, against today's planner numbers.
    3. Run the markdown plan in about 30 stores against 30 matched control stores for one clearance.
    4. Hand the top quality-gap lines to Quality and Buying, and track their return rates next season.

    Sample data: two synthetic seasons of Kidswear sales built to behave like real retail data, including stock-outs, a flat size curve, overbuying, rule-based clearance, quality defects and messy return comments. The hidden truth is used only to score results, never to train. Numbers here show the method works; they are not any retailer's results.
